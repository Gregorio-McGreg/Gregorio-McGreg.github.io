Nice going on your work so far; I'm sure Director Kash would be proud! It looks like you have indeed found the location where JAGCEC hid President Trump; you hear his moaning. A faint Orange glow seems to be coming from under some of the doors, but it's so strong that it's unclear where it's coming from. In order to find it, you must solve the following riddle to determine behind which door is President Trump.

There are 10 doors.
A door can be empty, be a trap, or contain President Trump.
Once you have deduced the correct door, go to its corresponding location.

  • Doors 1 and 9 are traps.
  • Door 3 is surrounded by empty rooms.
  • Both doors which are 2 away from 7 are traps.
  • Trump's door is after the pattern empty-trap. (meaning the whole pattern is empty-trap-Trump)
  • The 23 door is empty.
  • Trump's door has 3 as a factor.

Go to the corresponding location for the correct door, and you will find President Donald Trump.
